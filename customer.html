Get started

You don't need a separate connection request. Send the transaction details to the business owner and they can authorize it.

The business owner reviews the item, quantity, amount, and whether you intend to pay now or request pay-later credit. The request does not become a recorded sale or debt until the owner confirms it through the business workflow.
